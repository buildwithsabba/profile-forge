# Put your photo here

Upload it as **`photo.jpg`** in this folder.

`.png`, `.heic`, `.heif` and `.webp` work too — straight from an iPhone camera
roll is fine. Committing it starts the build.

**What converts well:** one face, looking at the camera, clearly lit, against a
plain or simple background. Harder lighting gives a better portrait than flat
lighting, because the ASCII ramp needs real highlights and shadows to work with.

**What does not:** group photos, full-body shots, heavy shadow across the face,
or a busy background. The pipeline removes the background and boosts local
contrast, but it cannot invent modelling that the photo never had.
